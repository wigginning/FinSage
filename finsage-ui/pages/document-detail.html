Item 7. Management’s Discussion and Analysis — For fiscal year 2024, net sales increased 2% or .6 billion compared to 2023, driven by higher iPhone, Services and Mac net sales, partially offset by lower iPad and Wearables, Home and Accessories net sales. Services net sales increased 13% to a record .2 billion, reflecting growth in App Store, advertising, AppleCare and cloud services.

Gross margin as a percentage of net sales was 46.2% in 2024 compared to 44.1% in 2023. The year-over-year increase was primarily due to a more favorable Services mix and improved leverage, partially offset by lower product margins. Operating expenses increased 6% to .0 billion in 2024, driven primarily by higher research and development spending.
